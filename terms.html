2. About StarSight

StarSight is an AI-assisted mobile application designed to support parents and teachers in observing a child's behavior and engagement while participating in play-based learning activities.

StarSight is developed and jointly managed by the StarSight Development Team, composed of Roxanne Oliveros, Christine Domat-ol, and Ron Cyrus Medina, under the Polytechnic University of the Philippines – Biñan Campus.

The application provides interactive learning experiences and behavioral observation features intended to help adults better understand how a child interacts with different activities.

StarSight includes the following learning categories:

  • Alphabet Forest: Activities involving letter introduction, pronunciation, and uppercase-to-lowercase matching.
  • Lumi Town: Story-based activities involving values, emotions, sharing, family, chores, and basic manners.
  • Arctic Numberland: Activities involving number tracing, number recognition, counting, and object identification.
  • Discovery Lagoon: Activities introducing basic concept awareness, including body parts, the five senses, weather, animals, food groups, and nature.
  • Puzzle Glade: Activities involving color sorting, pattern recognition, memory matching, puzzles, and object identification.

StarSight also provides parent and teacher features, including child profile management, behavioral analysis, progress monitoring, screen-time controls, and downloadable reports.

7. Camera Access and Behavioral Tracking

StarSight uses the device camera to observe the child's behavior while interacting with the learning activities.

Depending on the available features, the application may process:

  • Eye and gaze movement: To observe the child's visual attention toward active gameplay areas.
  • Head movement: To observe head orientation and movement during an activity.
  • Facial cues: To identify observable facial expressions during gameplay.
  • Screen interactions: To count the number of screen taps performed by the child.
  • Play duration: To determine the amount of time spent playing a particular game or level.

These observations are processed to generate behavioral indicators, including attention, focus, and engagement metrics.

StarSight allows the parent, guardian, or authorized teacher to set a screen-time (play-time) limit for the child.

  1. The screen-time limit may be set to a value between 15 and 60 minutes.
  2. When the set limit is reached, the game stops and a screen-limit screen is displayed.
  3. The responsible adult may allow the child additional play time (a "grace period") by entering the Parent PIN to unlock the screen-limit screen. Once unlocked, the child may play again.

The screen-time limit is a supplementary tool and does not replace the responsibility of parents, guardians, and teachers to monitor the child's overall screen use, to encourage breaks, and to decide whether additional play time is appropriate. StarSight does not guarantee that the limit will operate without error, for example due to device settings, application interruptions, or technical issues.

17. Third-Party Services and External Platforms

StarSight relies on third-party services and infrastructure to deliver its features. These services are used as follows:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google): For account registration, sign-in, session management, and password reset (including the sending of password-reset emails).
  • Google Sign-In: For Google account authentication, when selected by the user.
  • Cloud Firestore (Firebase): As StarSight's primary cloud database, for storing and synchronizing account information, child profiles, gameplay progress, behavioral metrics, and report data.
  • Microsoft Azure (virtual machine): To host StarSight's backend server. During gameplay, the application periodically sends camera frames to this server, where they are processed in real time to derive facial cue and behavioral metrics. The processed metrics are returned to the application and synchronized with Firestore.

  • Google Gemini API: To analyze tracked behavioral metrics and gameplay records after a session and generate the observational insights and summaries shown in reports. Generated analyses may be cached on the device for quick review.
  • Local device storage: To keep application settings on the device so that they persist across sessions and to reduce unnecessary network requests.

StarSight's source code is hosted and built using GitHub and GitHub Actions. These are development tools and are not intended to process users' personal information.
