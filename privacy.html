StarSight Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

StarSight ("we," "our," or "the Application") is an AI-assisted mobile application designed to support parents and teachers in observing children's learning participation and behavior through supervised, play-based learning activities.

StarSight is developed and jointly managed by the StarSight Development Team, composed of Roxanne Oliveros, Christine Domat-ol, and Ron Cyrus Medina, under the Polytechnic University of the Philippines – Biñan Campus.

The application provides interactive learning experiences and behavioral observation features that process certain information during a child's use of the application.

We recognize the importance of protecting the privacy and personal information of all our users, particularly children whose information may be processed through the application's behavioral tracking and analysis features.

This Privacy Policy explains how StarSight collects, uses, processes, stores, protects, retains, and manages personal information when users access and use the application.

This policy is guided by the principles and requirements of the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and applicable regulations and issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

By using StarSight, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. Where consent is the applicable legal basis for processing, the necessary consent is requested through the consent screen presented within the application and must be obtained before the relevant processing takes place.

2. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • Parents and legal guardians who register and manage StarSight accounts.
  • Teachers and other authorized adults who use the application.
  • Children whose profiles and behavioral information are processed through the application.
  • Individuals whose personal information is submitted, accessed, or processed through StarSight.

This policy covers information processed through the application's registration, authentication, child profile management, learning activities, behavioral tracking, analysis, reporting, screen-time management, and account management features.

It also covers information processed through the third-party services integrated into the application.

3. Information We Collect and Process

StarSight collects and processes information necessary to provide its learning and behavioral observation features.

The information processed may include the following categories:

3.1. Parent and Teacher Account Information

When an adult creates or manages an account, StarSight may collect:

  • Email address.
  • Authentication credentials or authentication-related information.
  • Four-digit Parent PIN, where applicable.
  • Account settings and preferences, including screen-time settings.
  • Account status and relevant account activity, including the date of last login (used to identify inactive accounts).

If a user chooses to register or sign in using a Google account, certain account information may be received through Google's authentication service, such as the email address and account identifier needed for authentication.

StarSight does not require users to provide unnecessary personal information beyond what is needed for the application's functions.

3.2. Child Profile Information

When a parent or authorized teacher creates a child profile, the application may process:

  • Child's nickname.
  • Date of birth or birthdate.
  • Gender.
  • Selected avatar.

This information is used to identify and manage the child's profile within the application and associate relevant learning activities and behavioral reports with the correct child.

3.3. Behavioral and Camera-Based Information

During supervised gameplay, StarSight uses the device camera to process observable behavioral information.

The application may analyze:

  • Eye and gaze movement.
  • Head movement and orientation.
  • Observable facial expressions or facial cues.

These observations are processed to derive behavioral indicators related to the child's attention, focus, and engagement while interacting with learning activities.

Important: Camera Processing and Recording Restrictions

StarSight's behavioral tracking functionality is designed for real-time processing. To avoid heavy processing on the child's device, camera frames are periodically sent from the device to StarSight's backend server (hosted on Microsoft Azure), where they are processed in real time to derive facial cue and behavioral metrics. The processed metrics are then returned to the application.

  • The application does not record or save videos of children for behavioral tracking.
  • The application does not capture and store photographs of children for behavioral tracking.
  • Camera frames sent to the backend are used only for real-time processing and are discarded immediately afterward, not stored.
  • Video and photo recordings are not saved to Firebase as part of the tracking feature.
  • Relevant processed behavioral metrics may be synchronized with Firebase for analysis and reporting.

Camera access is used for the stated behavioral observation functionality and is not intended for general surveillance or unrelated recording purposes.

3.4. Gameplay and Interaction Information

While a child participates in the learning activities, StarSight may process:

  • Number of screen taps.
  • Time spent playing each game or level.
  • Completed learning activities.
  • Gameplay progress.
  • Activity and session-related information.
  • Play time measured against the screen-time limit set by the parent or teacher.

This information helps the application monitor the child's interaction with learning activities, enforce screen-time limits, and support the generation of behavioral reports.

3.5. AI-Generated Behavioral Information

StarSight processes relevant behavioral and gameplay information to generate analytical indicators and reports.

These may include:

  • Attention metrics.
  • Focus metrics.
  • Engagement metrics.
  • Behavioral summaries.
  • Activity-related observations.

These outputs are generated through AI-assisted analysis and are intended to provide supplementary information for parents and teachers.

3.6. Technical and Operational Information

To maintain application functionality, StarSight and its supporting services may process certain technical information, such as:

  • Authentication and account status.
  • Application error information.
  • Network and synchronization status.
  • Technical information generated by integrated services.
  • Information necessary to maintain system security and troubleshoot technical issues.

The exact technical logs and metadata retained depend on the configuration of the application and its integrated service providers.

6. Camera Access and Real-Time Processing

Camera access is an important component of StarSight's behavioral observation functionality.

When a child begins an activity that requires behavioral tracking, the application may request permission to access the device camera.

The camera is used to observe the child's visible behavior during gameplay, including eye/gaze movement, head movement, and facial cues.

The tracking process is designed to operate as follows:

  1. The parent or teacher prepares the device and positions the child within the camera frame.
  2. The application accesses the camera after the necessary permission has been granted.
  3. Camera frames are periodically sent to StarSight's backend server, where they are processed in real time.
  4. The system derives relevant behavioral metrics, which are returned to the application.
  5. The processed metrics are synchronized with Firebase for analysis and reporting.
  6. The camera-based tracking process ends when the relevant activity or tracking session ends.

StarSight does not intend to retain raw camera recordings as part of its behavioral tracking feature.

Specifically:

  • Videos are not recorded or saved for behavioral tracking.
  • Photographs are not saved for behavioral tracking.
  • Camera frames are not stored on the backend server after processing.
  • Raw video or photo recordings are not saved to Firebase as part of the tracking process.
  • Processed behavioral metrics may be stored for reporting purposes.

The application requires camera permission to perform the tracking functionality. Users may manage camera permissions through their device settings. However, disabling camera access may prevent certain behavioral tracking features from functioning.

Camera permission is not intended to authorize unrelated camera use.

7. AI-Assisted Processing and Automated Analysis

StarSight uses AI-assisted and machine learning processes to interpret information obtained from the child's interaction with the application.

The system analyzes relevant behavioral indicators to produce attention, focus, and engagement metrics. Facial cue and gaze processing uses MediaPipe on StarSight's backend server, and the Google Gemini API is used to generate the written behavioral analysis from the tracked metrics.

The analysis may involve:

  • Eye and gaze movement patterns.
  • Head movement and orientation.
  • Observable facial cues.
  • Screen interaction frequency.
  • Time spent on individual learning activities.

The resulting information is used to generate behavioral reports for authorized parents and teachers.

Users should understand the following limitations:

  1. AI-generated indicators are estimates based on observable information.
  2. The system may produce incomplete results when tracking conditions are unsuitable.
  3. Facial expressions and eye movements cannot establish a child's actual thoughts or feelings.
  4. Looking away from the screen does not necessarily indicate a lack of attention.
  5. Behavioral indicators must not be interpreted as clinical or psychological conclusions.

StarSight does not intend to make medical, psychological, or developmental diagnoses through automated processing.

The application is designed to provide supplementary behavioral observations rather than make decisions that determine a child's rights, eligibility, educational placement, or access to services.

Where automated processing is used in a way that may significantly affect a data subject, the applicable legal requirements and safeguards must be observed.

8. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

StarSight uses third-party services to support account authentication, information synchronization, AI processing, and backend operations. The services used are:

8.1. Firebase (Google)

StarSight uses Firebase for:

  • Firebase Authentication: Account registration, sign-in, session management, and password reset.
  • Cloud Firestore: StarSight's primary cloud database, used for storing and synchronizing account information, child profile information, gameplay progress, behavioral metrics, and report data.

Firebase processes information according to its applicable service terms, privacy documentation, and the arrangements established by the application operator.

8.2. Google Authentication

Users may choose to authenticate through their Google accounts. When this option is selected, Google may provide the account information necessary to authenticate the user. Google's processing of personal information is subject to its own privacy policy and applicable terms.

8.3. Microsoft Azure

StarSight's backend server is hosted on a Microsoft Azure virtual machine. The server receives camera frames from the application during gameplay and processes them in real time to derive facial cue and behavioral metrics, which are returned to the application and synchronized with Firestore.

8.4. MediaPipe (Google)

StarSight uses MediaPipe, a computer vision framework and model, for face landmark detection (on the backend server) and gesture detection used in gameplay.

8.5. Google Gemini API

After a session, StarSight sends tracked behavioral metrics and gameplay records to the Google Gemini API to generate the observational insights and written summaries shown in reports. Generated analyses may be cached on the device for quick review.

8.6. Local Device Storage

StarSight stores application settings on the device so that they persist across sessions and to reduce unnecessary network requests.

8.7. Development Tools

StarSight's source code is hosted and built using GitHub and GitHub Actions. These are development tools and are not intended to process users' personal information.

These providers may process information on behalf of StarSight only as necessary for the services they provide and subject to appropriate contractual, technical, and organizational safeguards. StarSight does not authorize third-party providers to use children's information for unrelated purposes.

12. Data Deletion and Account Management

StarSight provides account management features that allow authorized users to manage their information.

Through the Parent's Area, users may:

  • Edit child profile information.
  • Permanently delete a child profile.
  • Change their account password.
  • Change their Parent PIN.
  • Log out of their account.
  • Permanently delete their account.

What happens when you delete your account

When you delete your account, we permanently delete your account information, your child's information, and all associated analysis from our active systems. Deleted data may remain in secure backups for up to 180 days before it is permanently removed. You can delete your account at any time from within the app.

Users should be aware that:

  1. Deleting a child profile may affect the availability of its associated behavioral reports.
  2. Deleting an account may result in the loss of access to associated profiles and reports.
  3. Information stored in backups remains for up to 180 days after deletion before it is purged.
  4. Certain information may be retained when required by law or necessary for the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims.

Downloading a PDF behavioral report creates a separate file outside the application's control. Users are responsible for deleting or securing their downloaded copies when they are no longer needed.

18. Screen Time and Child Well-Being

StarSight includes a parent management feature that allows users to configure a child's screen-time limit.

  • The parent or teacher can set the screen-time limit between 15 and 60 minutes.
  • When the limit is reached, the game stops and a screen-limit screen is shown.
  • The parent or teacher can allow the child additional play time (a grace period) by entering the Parent PIN to unlock the screen-limit screen, after which the child can play again.

The application is intended to support supervised learning rather than encourage excessive screen use.

Parents and teachers are responsible for determining appropriate screen-time limits based on the child's age, needs, and circumstances.

Information relating to gameplay duration and screen-time settings may be processed to apply the limit and to support progress monitoring and behavioral reporting.

StarSight encourages parents and teachers to provide appropriate breaks and stop activities whenever a child becomes uncomfortable or distressed.
